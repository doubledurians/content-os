# Reel Playbook

Distilled from Content Academy's "Graduating 1,000 View Jail" doc (15 short-form mistakes) plus what her own analytics show. Use as the checklist when reviewing a hook or script.

## Hook and idea
- Check topic words for demand (Google Trends, YouTube titles with 100K+ views). Pick the higher-demand phrasing.
- Hook summarizes the video in 10 words or less, clear and unique. Storytelling hooks need a shocking fact in the first 10 seconds.
- 10-year-old test: concrete everyday words ("YouTuber" over "content creator").
- Specifics over vagueness: numbers, names, dates ("45 days ago" beats "a while back").
- A statement isn't a hook until it creates tension or a question the viewer needs answered.

## Script
- Write for the viewer, not about yourself. Mirror their situation. Struggle relates; visible success alienates.
- One main point per video. Cut redundancy and tangents. If the viewer must remember 3 prior sentences, it's too wordy.
- Use re-hooks mid-video. Make sure the payoff delivers what the hook promised.
- Add one personal beat so it isn't a lecture.

## Delivery
- Avoid monotone. Practice with exaggerated range and dramatic pauses, do free-flow takes, try walking and talking.
- Jump cut every "um" and pause. Pacing should feel almost too fast. Record 3-4 sentences per take.

## Visuals and edit
- Do a "video breakdown" of top creators (shot length, shot type, effects, cuts per minute). Strong creators run 30-50 B-roll clips per minute, sometimes 5 in the first 5 seconds.
- Film B-roll habitually; keep a library of S-tier clips.
- Face centered, close, looking into the lens. Simple composition with a touch of interest.
- Match lighting and music to emotion. Add contrast, slight saturation, colored RGB/LED light.
- No fade transitions; intentional jump cuts.
- She wants a "sciency" editing style of her own (inspired by @sagecadence_'s artsy cyberpunk edits).

## What her own data says (as of early Nov 2026)
- Average skip rate ~50%. Target under 40%. **Hooks are the biggest lever.**
- Reels that open on her doing something perform best. "Chores + the Hawthorne effect" had the lowest skip rate (35.5%) and most views (6,003).
- Text-only motivation openers lost ~70% of viewers ("Get out of bed, open notes" 74.7%, "Trial reel plea" 74.1%).
- Other top reels: "Can you actually grow your brain?" (4,479), "Where you're at v4" (4,271, 50 saves), "Study spot (washing machine)" (3,964, 18 shares).
- Shares and saves are the strongest reach signals and what brands look at. A reference reel with 4,132 shares on 22.6K likes showed practical numbered-list psych tips get shared heavily.

## Reading metrics
- **Skip rate** (swipe in first 3s) grades the hook.
- **Avg watch time** grades the body and re-hooks; compare to reel length.
- **Trial reels** go to non-followers; compare with each other only.
