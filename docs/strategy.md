## Positioning

**Three-word niche:** everyday psychology explained.
Alternatives considered: "applied cognitive science" (more credible, less searched), "psychology of decisions" (if You Got Played becomes the main series).

**Follow promise (locked 2026-11-10, see `content-master-strategy.md`):** Follow me and you'll stop getting played by your own brain, and learn how to make it work for you.

**Earlier follow pitch:** Follow me if you want to understand your own brain better, from the psychology hiding in everyday choices and the products you use to the science you can actually apply to your life, all told through the lens of a cog sci student living it out in LA.

**Her edge:** she actually studies this (cog sci at UCLA, first short study published in a journal in 2026), plus a startup/VC lens from Bruin Ventures and Bruin Strategy Network. Lean on "because of what I know about the brain" style credibility.

## Pillars (priority order)

1. **Consumer tech through a human-behavior lens** (psych tricks inside products, startups worth using)
2. **Applied psychology** (biases, unconscious decisions, study science, brain questions)
3. **UCLA / LA lifestyle** (occasional; best when it carries one line of psychology)

## Format decisions

- **Core:** zero lead time videos (test the viewer, cog sci in the wild, talking head + bold text + word-by-word captions).
- Friends as subjects occasionally.
- **Dropped (Nov 2026):** multi-week self-experiment series. Too much lead time, hard to measure.
- Multi-part explainers work (optogenetics, startup picks).
- Uses Instagram **Trial Reels** to test hooks with non-followers. Compare trials with each other, not with feed reels.
