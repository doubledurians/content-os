## 1. Who is my IVP (ideal viewer persona)?

**MinoAI Frontier's method:** pick *one real person*, ideally someone you know or a younger you, with a name and a date attached. Not an age range. "18 to 24" is two different people with different problems, and even one college student's priorities flip between finals season and the rest of the quarter. Write down their pain points *and* their objections to your content. ([Ideal Viewer Persona Breakdown](https://www.loom.com/share/1079d20364934ddb907a0bc6de463dc3), [Ideal Viewer Persona Lesson 4/14](https://www.loom.com/share/44aa31a9824244e897d0f310f66b2a44))

**IVP: "Abi, sophomore year"** **LOCKED 2026-11-10:** younger me, sophomore year at UCLA (2025-26).

A 20-year-old at a big university (think UCLA, sophomore/junior year).
- Smart, curious, takes a "nerdy" major or wishes they did. Into tech, startups and AI, maybe wants a PM / VC / tech job, but isn't an engineer.
- Overcommitted: classes, a club or two, a part-time job, trying to have a social life.
- Knows their phone is eating their day, procrastinates on the thing that matters, then crams.
- Consumes a lot of self-improvement and productivity content and is a bit tired of it. It makes them feel guilty, not smarter.
- Wants to feel like they're the one in control: of their attention, their study time, their money, and the apps they use.

**Pain points (what's top of mind)**
1. "I lose hours on my phone and I don't even enjoy it."
2. "I study a lot and still forget everything / still cram."
3. "I keep making choices I regret (buying, scrolling, saying yes) and don't know why."
4. "Everyone online is selling me a routine. I just want to know what actually works."
5. "I want to sound smart about tech and startups without a CS degree." (career anxiety)

**Objections they'll have to my content** (each one is a script check)
- "This is pop psych / fake neuroscience." → Answer with sources, and with the fact that I study it.
- "I already know apps are addictive." (The Social Dilemma effect) → Win on mechanism plus a takeaway they didn't have.
- "Another productivity girl telling me to wake up at 5am." → No guilt. Explain, don't preach.
- "You're just a student." → A student is exactly who they trust on student life; the published study and lab/VC experience cover the rest.

**Seasonal shifts to plan around:** weeks 9-10 and finals (study, stress, sleep), start of quarter (habits, resets), Black Friday / Wrapped / New Year (product psychology moments).

## 2. The one clear reason my first 10k should follow me

**Locked promise (2026-11-10):** *Follow me and you'll stop getting played by your own brain, and learn how to make it work for you.* (First draft was option A below.)

Plain version for a follow CTA she might say out loud: "If you want to understand why you do what you do, and catch it before it costs you, follow me."

Why this one:
- It unifies both pillars. Biases and habits (applied psych) and dark patterns (consumer tech) are the same story: your brain has defaults and something is pulling on them.
- "You Got Played" already carries the language, so the series *is* the brand promise.
- It's viewer-centric (their problem), not topic-centric ("I talk about psychology").

### Where tools that actually help fit (added 2026-11-10)

The promise is about who's in control, not about tech being bad. Getting played means something uses your brain's defaults against you. The flip side is using the same defaults for you. So helpful tools aren't an exception to the niche; they're the payoff.

- **Same lens, different verdict:** every product reel answers one question, "does this work with your brain or against it?" Example: a streak can guilt you into opening an app, while spaced repetition uses how memory works to help you learn. [fact-check specifics before filming]
- Learning tools, Startup Picks, HeyClicky, Wispr Flow: frame each around the psychology that makes it genuinely help. That keeps them inside the brand instead of reading as product reviews.
- MinoAI Frontier: a unique through-line matters more than a rigid niche, and a short coined phrase people can attach to you makes a brand stick ([Coaching Call 3/18](https://www.loom.com/share/f945068d487149cf98d6a77c593d9adc), [Mino's Coaching Call 3/17](https://www.loom.com/share/f39f1cda9a6c4aa582768ce080eaa35f)). Idea: give the positive verdict its own recurring line, the way "you got played" names the negative one. **[her call: pick the wording]**
- **DECIDED 2026-11-10: no separate positive catchphrase.** A line like "so now you're the one playing" needs series context, and most viewers are strangers seeing one reel. Every recurring line has to make sense cold.
  - **Default ending for positive reels:** end on the promise itself, "...and that's how you make your brain work for you." Swap the first half per reel, keep the ending (e.g. "...that's how you make your brain work for you during finals"; "once you know what the habit's asking for, your brain starts working for you").
  - **Product reels only:** set up the contrast inside the same reel so the payoff has context, e.g. "Duolingo's streak plays you. This one actually works for you."
  - **Repetition comes from** the same ending structure plus a visual stamp (same card + sound): red for "played," green for "works for you."
- **Promise wording options [her call]:**
  - (A) as drafted: "Follow me and you'll stop getting played by your own brain, and by the apps built to exploit it."
  - (B) extended: "Follow me and you'll stop getting played by your own brain and your apps, and learn how to make them work for you."
  - **(C) brain-first, LOCKED 2026-11-10:** "Follow me and you'll stop getting played by your own brain, and learn how to make it work for you."
    - Covers non-tech reels (therapist post-it habits, loss aversion, Hawthorne chores, study tips, Costco) without stretching, since most of the 80% bucket isn't about tech.
    - Apps become one of the things that play your brain (You Got Played); helpful tools sit under "make it work for you."
    - Tradeoff: less distinctive than A, so the tech/founder edge lives in the bio and YGP instead of the promise.
  - The promise is the reason to follow, not a line to say in every reel. A reel only has to deliver on it.

**MinoAI Frontier on getting to 10k:** for the first 10k, farm value content for one IVP and repeat the format that works. Most creators get roughly 80% of their followers from one style of video. ([COACHING CALL 2/24](https://www.loom.com/share/381c07bf3c9241258759fff6e39f0a42), [Coaching Call 2026-05-06](https://www.loom.com/share/ad2c38ccce2b4131a8b48cc74511bdbb?t=3262s))

**What this means for the mix until 10k (proposal):**
- ~80%: **"caught in the act"** reels. She's doing something everyday, names the psychology behind it, gives the viewer one thing to do or notice. (Hawthorne chores reel is the template. You Got Played fits here when it opens on her using the product.)
- ~20%: tests and personality (Sidequests recap, UCLA life, Science Deep Dives).
- **Startup Picks:** honest pushback. It's her lowest performer and it speaks to a different person (someone shopping for tools, not someone trying to understand their brain). Suggest pausing it as a standalone series until 10k, and folding good startups into YGP as the "here's one built the right way" contrast. **[her call]**

> Clash flag: MinoAI Frontier's general advice is to post daily for 30 days before judging. Her set capacity is 3-4 reels a week. Keeping 3-4/week is fine; just judge formats over ~5 weeks instead of 30 days.

## 3. How the brand solves specific problems for that one viewer

Each pain point gets a repeatable content answer.

| Her viewer's problem | What my content gives them | Series / format |
|---|---|---|
| Phone eats my day | The exact mechanism being used on them (variable rewards, streaks, infinite scroll) + one way to break it | You Got Played |
| Study a lot, still forget | Evidence-based study methods, tested on my own exams | Study Smarter ("3 things I do before every exam because of what I know about the brain") |
| Choices I regret | Name the bias in a real moment (Costco haul, Black Friday timer, loss aversion) | Caught in the act / In the Wild |
| Tired of guru content | Explanations with sources, no guilt, one takeaway | Tone rule for everything |
| Want to be fluent in tech | How products are designed to change behavior, from someone who's built one and sat in VC | You Got Played (business layer at the end) |

**Every reel should pass this check:** which of the five problems does it solve, and would "Abi, sophomore year" send it to a friend? If neither, it's a 20% reel or a Parking Lot idea.

## 5. My story and how it makes my way of serving viewers different

**MinoAI Frontier on story:** strangers don't care what happened to you unless it pertains to them, so flip the story onto the viewer. Personal stories ground the concept and are what make a personal brand personal. ([Coaching Call 2026-05-06](https://www.loom.com/share/ad2c38ccce2b4131a8b48cc74511bdbb?t=3262s), [CHAPTER 8: LIST Video Tutorial, Scripting Part 1](https://whop.com/joined/contentacademy/content-bootcamp-3pqyjhwaG9gmeC/app/courses/cors_7pWrAMvSU9J9TvKJP0c8tK/lessons/lesn_3WlLYk5qhPNL6AYGfkjVXQ/))

**Draft origin story (three beats)** **[her call: rewrite in her words]**
1. **I was the one getting played.** Overcommitted (four jobs at once), procrastinating, phone always in hand, studying hard and still cramming. Same as you.
2. **Then I saw both sides.** In cog sci I learned *why* my brain was doing that. Building SquadUp and working in VC, I saw the other side: products are designed around exactly those habits, on purpose.
3. **So now I translate.** I'm not here to tell you to delete your apps or wake up at 5am. I show you what's going on under the hood so you get to choose.

**How the story changes the way she serves viewers:**
- She explains from **both sides of the screen**: the user's brain and the builder's incentives. That's the gap between her and both psych creators and tech creators.
- She's **in it with them** (student, same apps, same finals), so the "we" and "I" beats are honest, not a pose. (Matches house rule 7.)
- She **tests on herself first**, so advice comes with a personal result, which MinoAI Frontier calls the minimum for credibility.

**Where the story shows up:**
- A pinned "who I am and why I make this" reel built on the three beats above (the Oct 8 intro reel did 1.5x usual; a sharper version with the promise could be the pinned follow-converter).
- Bio. Draft modelled on MinoAI Frontier's bio structure (name, proof, offer, CTA): **[her call]**
  - `abi | cog sci @ UCLA, published researcher`
  - `why your brain does that (and how apps use it)`
  - `new reel every 2-3 days ↓`
- One line of the story in every ~5th reel (a mini callback, not the whole arc).
